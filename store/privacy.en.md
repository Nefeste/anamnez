# Anamnez — Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

Anamnez is an Android game by Gornitsa (gornitsa.games). This page explains what data the
game collects. In short: **none**.

## What the game collects and shares

Nothing. The game has no internet permission, so it physically cannot send anything to
the developer or anyone else. There are no ads, analytics, trackers, accounts or
purchases.

## What stays on your phone

Game saves (the current shift, patients, daily results) are stored in the app's private
storage on your phone. The developer has no access to them.

If Android backup is turned on, the system may keep a copy of the game's data in your
Google account, as it does for other apps. That is done by Android, not by the game, and
the developer has no access to that copy.

## If you contact us yourself

If you choose to write to the developer — by email, or via "Share" in the game once an
error report is added — we only see what you send and use it only to reply and fix bugs.
We do not pass such messages to third parties.

## Permissions

- **Vibrate** — touch feedback and the urgent-patient alert.
- **Modify audio settings** — the game's sound volume.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings: Settings → Apps → Anamnez →
Storage → Clear data.

## Age

The game is intended for ages 16 and up.

## Medical disclaimer

Anamnez is a game. Its medical content is simplified for gameplay; it is not a reference,
a self-diagnosis tool or a substitute for a doctor. If you are worried about your health,
see a doctor; in an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Russia and the
EU).

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
